Capture

Win the contract. Registration, positioning, and bid preparation.

Most small businesses lose government work in the setup, before the bid. Capture fixes it. We correct your registrations, position you in front of the right buyers, and build a bid that clears review.

Best for

  • Businesses new to government contracting
  • Companies registered but never awarded
  • Set-aside eligible firms
  • Subcontractors ready to prime
01

Registration and compliance setup

SAM.gov, eVA, and state and local portals, plus NAICS and NIGP selection.

02

Market and opportunity research

We identify the agencies buying your services, the vehicles they use, and the incumbents holding the work.

03

Capability statement and positioning

A one-page capability statement built the way contracting officers read.

04

Bid preparation and response

Proposal drafting, compliance matrix, pricing narrative, and a review pass before submission.

02

Continuity

Keep operations running. Embedded, fractional leadership.

Some operations do not need a full-time executive. They need an experienced one on the days that matter. Continuity places senior leadership inside your organization on a set cadence, with real authority and accountability.

Best for

  • Owners running operations on top of another job
  • Organizations between operations hires
  • Nonprofits scaling past founder-led operations
  • First-award firms with no admin infrastructure
01

Fractional operations leadership

A senior operator in your business on a set schedule. Standing meetings, live decisions, documented follow-through.

02

Process and SOP build

We document how the work gets done so it survives turnover and audit.

03

Contract and program administration

Deliverable tracking, reporting calendars, subcontractor coordination, and compliance follow-through on current awards.

04

Team development and handoff

We build the internal capacity to take the work back. The goal is a clean handoff.

03

Sprint

Ninety days to a target win.

Sprint is Capture compressed and aimed at one target. We choose the opportunity, build what the bid requires, and submit inside ninety days.

Best for

  • Firms with an opportunity in sight
  • Businesses that need a deadline to move
  • Companies that registered and stalled
  • Owners learning the process with support
01

Weeks 1 to 2: Target selection

We assess your registrations, past performance, and capacity, then choose one winnable opportunity.

02

Weeks 3 to 5: Readiness build

Registrations corrected, capability statement finalized, teaming conversations opened.

03

Weeks 6 to 10: Bid build

Technical narrative, compliance matrix, pricing, and required forms assembled against the solicitation.

04

Weeks 11 to 12: Review and submit

Full compliance review, a red team pass, and submission ahead of the deadline.

The deliverable is a submitted, compliant, competitive bid. The award decision belongs to the agency.
